What makes it different?

Everyday browsing tools and AI shortcuts are brought together in one simple interface.

🤖

AI Quick Access

Open supported AI websites directly from the browser dashboard. Sign-in and account access remain with each service.

◫

Split-Screen AI Compare

View two AI tools side-by-side to compare their answers without constantly switching tabs.

🛡️

Ad & Tracker Filtering

Optional built-in content-blocking rules can block selected ad and tracking hosts and hide some common ad containers. It may not block every ad.

🕶️

Incognito Tabs

Open a separate private tab from the tab manager. Incognito is not invisibility: websites, network providers, and signed-in services may still identify activity.

▣

Clear Tab Manager

See open tabs, switch between them, close individual tabs, or create a new normal or incognito tab.

🔎

Search Engine Choice

Use the available search engine options, including Google, Bing, and DuckDuckGo.

🌙

Dark & Light Themes

Choose the appearance that feels comfortable for your browsing environment.

📱

Designed for Android

A mobile-first interface with a home dashboard, navigation controls, and quick access to common browser actions.
